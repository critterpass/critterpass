3Critterpass: every screen, 67 in all
A guide for every place
Critterpass plans group trips with an AI guide that lives at the destination. Each place has its own creature with a colour and a voice. The guide drafts the plan, pitches it to the crew, runs the day, and ends up on your pass once you've been there.
HOW TO READ THIS
Two trips run through it. The Bali Six are 17 days out from Bali, which covers planning, money and the trip itself. They are also voting on the next one: Kyoto wins and goes through setup and the proposal.
Anything that moves on a screen is live. The line under each phone describes its motion and how it moves to the next screen.
Critters can't be bought. You earn them by being somewhere, and encounters reward staying put.
