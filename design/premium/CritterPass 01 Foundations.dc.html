1.A · Navigation grammarFive screen types, one rule eachWhat kind of screen each one is decides its header buttons. Every phone in parts 2–9 lists its ways out or every tap underneath, so you can trace that nothing dead-ends.
Root · large title
Home, Trips, Wallet, Pass
Tab bar shows; no back button.
Right side: up to two glass actions, + is the dark one.
Title collapses into the bar on scroll.
Push · inline title
Details, settings, lists
Glass chevron and edge swipe go back.
One right action: share, ⋯ or Edit.
Tab bar hides on details, so the content gets the height.
CancelDone
Sheet · grabber
Forms, pickers, compose
Cancel left, the bold verb right (Post, Save, Add).
Swipe down cancels. With edits, it asks first.
Half height for one choice; full height for a form.
Full screen · ✕
Camera, boarding pass, show mode
Only for one-job moments: scanning, showing, celebrating.
✕ top-left on glass. Never a second cover on top.
Screen brightens for passes and phrase cards.
Alert · two choices
Destructive or blocking
The question as the title, the result as the line.
Destructive in pink, Cancel always there.
Can’t undo it? Hold to confirm instead of tapping.
Three ways forward
Every error gives a retry, another route, and a way back. Nothing ends on a bare message.
Offline never blocks reading
Saved trips, passes and phrases open without signal. Changes wait in a queue you can see.
Nothing typed is lost
Failed sends keep their text. Dismissing a sheet with changes asks first. Drafts come back.
Roles shape the screen
Members see read-only plans with a Nudge. The organiser sees the controls. Solo trips skip the vote.
