How CritterPass works, for help articles. Only state what is here; never promise anything else.

- CritterPass plans group trips for a crew: a guide critter drafts the plan, the crew votes, and the plan updates as people join.
- Six live guides cover Bali (Tokek), Kyoto (Pon), Iceland (Lundi), Mexico City (Ajo), Lisbon (Sardi) and Cusco (Paco). Anywhere else, Tokek covers as a guest guide.
- The guide is an AI and can be wrong. Free accounts can ask 30 guide questions a day; the count resets at midnight on the phone.
- CritterPass is never the merchant of record. Stays are booked on partner sites through links; CritterPass never holds a room. Some activities can be booked in the app through the partner (Viator), which holds the booking; refunds and changes follow that partner's policy and are requested from the partner. CritterPass does not book flights.
- Money: the crew splits costs inside the app as a shared ledger (who paid, who owes). CritterPass does not move money between people; people settle up however they like.
- Pass+ is a subscription ($3.99 a month or $29.99 a year) bought through the App Store or Google Play; a Trip Boost (about $12) unlocks extras for one trip and can be split in the ledger. Refunds for these go through Apple or Google.
- Critters are found by being at places (sometimes at certain times, or together with the crew). Critters can't be bought, sold or traded. Legendary forms show up only in their window or for the hardest thing a place has.
- Maps and the trip pack (plan, places, phrase cards, emergency numbers) work offline once downloaded; changes made offline send when the phone is back online.
- Location is only used on trip days, and CritterPass never keeps a trail of where you went. Crew location sharing is opt-in and time-limited.
- Safety: the Help screen shows local emergency numbers and nearby hospitals and pharmacies; in an emergency call the local number first. Insurance guidance is general; CritterPass does not sell or recommend an insurer.
- Account: you can export your data or delete your account from Settings; deletion has a 30-day grace period.
- A human replies to feedback by email within two days.
