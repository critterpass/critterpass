- **Target:** `researcher-260926-1143-native-platform-monetization-report.md` (not edited)
- **Date:** 2026-09-26. **Method:** Apple DocC JSON (`developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/...`), raw App Review Guidelines HTML, Apple Developer News, Android/Play/Firebase docs via WebFetch, vendor pricing pages, `gh release list`, Discord Zendesk API.
- **Limitation:** the WebSearch budget for this session was already used up (200/200), so every check was a direct fetch of a primary URL. Claims with no fetchable primary source are marked **uncertain**.
- **Verdict summary:** 31 claims checked.
  - 27 confirmed (several with small corrections).
  - 2 outdated.
  - 2 uncertain.
  - 0 refuted.
- **The recommendation still holds.** 12 omissions are listed in §2. The ones that matter most are the EU alternative payments (from 2026-10-01), the iPhone Duo foldable (2026-10-23), the Apple social-media age-rating / Time Allowances rule, guideline 5.1.5's clause on emergency services, and Android 17 background audio.

## 2. Omissions (what the report missed)

1. **EU alternative payments from 2026-10-01** (Apple News 2026-08-18). EU App Store apps may offer alternative payment options alongside IAP, under a single new set of business terms. The Core Technology Fee is replaced by a 5% Core Technology Commission for apps distributed outside the App Store. The report covers only the US link-out case, but Iceland and Lisbon are core destinations, so EU users are likely. Add this to decision 12.
2. **iPhone Duo** (foldable, two displays; ships 2026-10-23 on iOS 27.1; Xcode 27.1). The design is drawn only at 390×844. Adaptive layouts plus widget, LA and Dynamic Island behaviour on Duo are unassessed.
3. **Apple social-media age rating + Time Allowances (iOS 27).** The questionnaire is mandatory for submissions from Sept 2026. If an app has social-media capabilities (redistributing or interacting with UGC via a feed), it gets a minimum 13+ rating and falls in the Social Media Time Allowance category. Critterpass's 3o community feed, 3p idea board and published crew plans likely qualify. Related: Declared Age Range API obligations (Utah 2026-05-06, Louisiana 2026-07-01, plus BR/AU/SG).
4. **Guideline 5.1.5:** "Location-based APIs shouldn't be used to provide emergency services". The 3k help/emergency tiles, crew SOS and the 1-h location share must be framed as crew coordination, not an emergency service. Nothing may imply dispatch or rescue.
5. **Android 17 background audio hardening (target 37).** Background audio requires a WIU FGS, **or** an exact-alarm grant plus `USAGE_ALARM` streams. The report's degrade path (exact alarm denied) must not play alarm audio from the background.
6. **LA animation is more capable than stated** (claim 8). Spring or hop transitions of ≤2 s per update are allowed on iOS 17+. This improves the gecko-trail fidelity plan.
7. **The LPSE entitlement likely requires an Apple request** (claim 18). Add lead time to the crew-map plan.
8. **The AlarmKit secondary intent is available only after first unlock** (claim 6). Snooze-once is fine in normal use, but alarms right after a reboot degrade.
9. **RevenueCat In-App Currency** could hold unused Boost credits (they must not expire under 3.1.1). This lowers own-ledger work. Trip binding stays server-side.
10. **Apple "Monthly subscriptions with a 12-month commitment"** is now worldwide. It is a possible Crew Year / Pass+ pricing variant the matrix didn't consider.
11. **Apple positions multiseat Group Purchases for "collaborative groups".** That is a possible crew-seat Pass+ path on iOS (decision 13 currently recommends opting out). The 2026-10-22 opt-out deadline only matters once products exist in ASC.
12. **Deadlines:** iOS 27 SDK (Xcode 27) is required for submissions from **April 2027**, which does not change the deployment target. Play targetSdk/PBL extensions run to 2026-11-01. Play reduced "Apps Experience" rate cards start 2026-09-30.

## 3. Does the recommendation hold?

**Yes.** Every load-bearing pillar is confirmed against primary sources:
- iOS 26 minimum, based on AlarmKit, scheduled LA start and WidgetKit push being iOS 26+, plus 79%/86% adoption.
- Broadcast channels for fan-out.
- The lock-screen authentication caveat.
- The When-In-Use session default with an Always upgrade.
- All-IAP monetization, with a ledger-only IOU and Offer Codes for partners.
- RevenueCat, on pricing and SDK activity.
- Opting out of Group Purchases, and decoupling the postcard.

Nothing was refuted. Amendments to fold into the plan:
- (a) Add EU alternative-payment and age-rating/social-media items to the policy decisions.
- (b) Add iPhone Duo layout scope.
- (c) Soften the "no animation" wording to "no continuous animation; ≤2 s per-update animations".
- (d) Add LPSE entitlement lead time.
- (e) Add the Android 17 audio caveat to the alarm degrade path.
- (f) Correct the Play external-link savings wording.
