HOW IT HOLDS TOGETHER
1
Data first. A guide appears only on Home and when a screen is empty, blocked or broken.
2
Colour means time and health: pink for past SLA or down, orange for close or degraded. Queue types stay neutral.
3
Verdicts and user approvals get stamps, the same way the app stamps a passport.
4
Every write shows its diff first and leaves an audit row with an op_id.
For the code: add work, billing, content, community, services, operators to ADMIN_AREAS. The six seed operators and all numbers are sample data.
