TL;DR FROM TOKEK
THE SHORT VERSION
✓
We use your location to hatch critters and plan your days. You can switch it off any time.
✓
Your crew sees the trip. Nobody else does unless you share it.
✓
We don't sell your data. Delete your account and it goes, trips and all.
01
WHAT WE COLLECT

Your name, email and profile photo when you sign up. The trips, votes, plans, messages and receipts you add to a crew. Your location while the app is in use or while a trip is active, if you allow it.

We also collect basic device and usage information so we can fix bugs and see which features people use.

02
LOCATION AND THE PASS

Critters hatch when you are actually somewhere, so the pass needs your location to work. We check it when the app is open, and during an active trip if you turn on travel mode.

We store the places you have been as pass entries, not a trail of coordinates. You can turn location off in settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

03
TALKING TO YOUR GUIDE

When you ask a guide something, by text or voice, we process it to answer you and to update the plan. Voice clips are turned into text and then deleted.

Guide conversations are part of your trip. Your crew can see what the guide changed in the plan, not your private questions.

04
WHO SEES WHAT

Everyone in a crew sees that trip: the plan, the votes, shared expenses and the guide’s suggestions. People outside the crew see nothing unless you send them an invite or share a link.

Your pass is private by default. You can make it visible to friends.

05
PARTNERS

When you book a stay, activity or transfer through Critterpass, we pass the booking partner the details needed to complete it. Payments are handled by our payment processor; we never see your full card number.

We do not sell personal data or share it with advertisers.

06
KEEPING AND DELETING

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account we remove your profile, pass and private messages within 30 days. Shared trip items you added stay with the crew with your name removed.

07
YOUR RIGHTS

You can download a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it from Settings → Privacy. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights, and we honour them wherever you are.

08
CHANGES

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Questions about any of this? Write to hello@critterpass.app and a human will answer.
