HOW SOMEONE GETS IN
1
Allow-list their email
Add it to ADMIN_ALLOWLIST in the api environment and redeploy.
2
They sign in
Cloudflare Access first, then Google with that work email.
3
You give them a role
Until then they can sign in but see nothing.
Removing someone: clear their roles here first, which ends their sessions at once. Then take them off the allow-list.
IF THE CONSOLE IS DOWN
Owners can run any command from a laptop with the emergency CLI. It writes the same audit rows.
pnpm admin:cmd revoke_session '{…}'
