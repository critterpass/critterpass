Critter collection: 150 locals across 61 places
One local per city
Every city in the collection has one critter, and you get it by being there. Vietnam is the home set with ten. The ten most-visited places get five cities each, places 11 to 30 get three, and places 31 to 60 get one. Each name comes from the local word for the animal, or the nickname locals already use.
