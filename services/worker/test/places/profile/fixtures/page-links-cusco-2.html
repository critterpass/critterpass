## Sacred Valley Trips & Tours from Cusco There are day tripsfrom Cusco that combine both Písac and Ollantaytambo, two of the main Inca sites in the Cusco region. Visiting these areas on the same day is a great way to maximise your time. We chose to hire a private driver from Cusco so we could see the specific sights we wanted during our trip. ## Moray [...] We chose to board the train in Ollantaytambo and made the scenic journey up to Machu Picchu in 1.5 hours. We set off early from Cusco and spent the day exploring sights in both Pisac and Ollantaytambo before boarding the train. This meant we were spending the night in Aguas Calientes and had citadel tickets for Machu Picchu the next morning. Unless you wish to hike, remember to purchase bus tickets from Aguas Calientes up to the citadel as these are not included in your entry tickets. [...] ## Písac Market & Ruins Driving 30-40 minutes from Cusco, over the hills and into the Sacred Valley of the Incas, lies the town of Písac, a popular place to visit on day trips. It’s most famous for colourful textile markets and picturesque hilltop Inca ruins. Narrow streets are filled with ceramics, souvenirs, textiles, jewellery and crafts. There are also many restaurants and food stalls. It is open every day, but Sundays see the largest number of vendors.
