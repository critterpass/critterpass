Bao Dai Summer Palace

Bao Dai Summer Palace, also called Dinh III, was the summer residence of Vietnam's last emperor, Bao Dai. Built between 1933 and 1938, the villa sits on a pine-covered hill in Da Lat.

The entire palace is divided into two floors, containing 25 rooms that preserve the original furniture, royal items, and many precious historical photographs from that era.

To protect the historical floors, visitors must wear shoe covers provided by the park.

It gets busy with tour groups from late morning; arrive early or come after mid-afternoon for a quieter visit. Allow about an hour to see the rooms and the gardens.
