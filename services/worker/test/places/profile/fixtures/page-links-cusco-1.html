The train journey from Cusco to Machu Picchu takes no more than 4 hours, which can be an advantage if you have limited time to visit this monument. Within the city, there are several stations that operate this route, although some are used more frequently than others. Discover the options that the Imperial City has to offer! [...] The journey begins with a car ride from Cusco to the small town of Santa María, which takes about 4 hours. After that, another 30-minute drive is needed to reach Santa Teresa, followed by a 30-minute trip to Hidroléctrica. At this point, you can choose to walk for 3 hours to get to Aguas Calientes or board a train that takes 30 minutes
