Third-party notices for @cp/critter-art
=========================================

design/doodles.js (the Critterpass design prototype's hand-drawn renderer) adapts the brush-pen
stroke, wash and palette approach from alesha-pro/tools (skills/hand-drawn-canvas-animation),
which is MIT-licensed. This package ports that renderer's math and drawing logic to TypeScript,
so the same notice carries into the following files:

  src/core/rng.ts
  src/core/spline.ts
  src/core/ribbon.ts
  src/core/ops.ts
  src/core/shapes.ts (ellipsePolygon)
  src/kinds/guides/gecko.ts
  src/kinds/guides/tanuki.ts
  src/kinds/guides/puffin.ts
  src/kinds/guides/axolotl.ts
  src/kinds/guides/sardine.ts
  src/kinds/guides/alpaca.ts
  src/kinds/icons/badges.ts
  src/kinds/icons/objects.ts
  src/kinds/icons/scenes.ts
  src/kinds/icons/weather.ts
  src/kinds/icons/annotations.ts
  src/kinds/parts/face.ts (eyes, cheeks, extras, toes)
