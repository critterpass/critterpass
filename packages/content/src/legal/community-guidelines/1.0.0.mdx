## Crews

Crews are small and private. Treat people in yours the way you would on the trip itself: no harassment, threats or hate, and no pressuring anyone to pay, go or share more than they want.

## Sharing plans and photos

When you publish a plan or a recap, it can be seen by anyone with the link. Share only what you took or have permission to share, and leave out other people's faces and names unless they agreed. The app hides names and blurs faces by default.

## Places and locals

Respect the places you go: follow local rules, don't encourage trespassing or anything unsafe, and don't post reviews or tips you haven't lived.

## Not allowed

Illegal content, sexual content involving anyone under 18, violence, scams, spam, impersonation, and selling anything through the app.

## Reporting

Press and hold any message, photo or plan to report it. A person on our team reviews every report. We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these guidelines, and we tell you why.
