## Guides are AI

Tokek, Pon, Lundi, Ajo, Sardi, Paco and the other guides are characters powered by AI models. They are not people and never pretend to be. Messages and plan changes they write are labelled as theirs.

## What they do with your words

To answer, a guide receives your question and the parts of your trip it needs (places, dates, the plan), never private details like your phone number. The AI providers we use process this for us and may not train their models on it.

## Where they can be wrong

Guides draft plans, suggest places and answer questions. They can get opening hours, prices, routes or local rules wrong. For anything important, like visas, health, safety or money, check an official source. Tips on this site are drafted with help from AI and checked by people before they are published.

## You stay in charge

A guide proposes changes to a plan; your crew sees what changed and anyone can undo it. You can turn guide suggestions down, and choose plain text instead of the guide's handwriting in settings.
