## What you can buy

**Pass+** unlocks the guide without daily limits, every icon style and pass extras. Monthly or yearly.

**Trip Boost** unlocks the live crew map and extra guide help for one trip. It is a one-off purchase. If the trip is cancelled, the boost moves to the crew's next trip; boosts never expire.

**Crew yearly** gives the buyer Pass+ everywhere and Boost on every trip of one crew for a year. It is bound to one crew when you buy it and can be moved to another crew once per year.

Prices are shown in the app in your store's currency before you pay.

## Auto-renewal

Pass+ and Crew yearly renew automatically at the end of each period at the same price, unless you turn auto-renewal off at least 24 hours before the period ends. Your store account is charged within 24 hours before renewal.

## Cancelling

Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. You keep what you paid for until the end of the period. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a store subscription on its own.

## Refunds

Purchases are made with Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them under their policies. We can't issue refunds ourselves.

## Changes to prices

If a price changes, your store tells you before the new price applies to a renewal, and you can cancel before then.
