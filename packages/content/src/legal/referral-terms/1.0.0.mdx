## How a stamp is earned

Share your link from You → Invite friends, or add a friend straight to a crew. A stamp lands for both of you once your friend has joined and either voted on or planned their first trip.

## What stamps unlock

One stamp gives the Tagalong badge, three the Navy cover and five the Collector cover. Stamps and covers are cosmetic, have no cash value and can't be transferred or exchanged.

## Fair play

A friend has to be new to CritterPass, and each friend earns one stamp. Stamps from duplicate or fake accounts are removed. Critters still only come from visiting places.

## Changes

We may change or end the programme. Stamps and covers you already earned stay on your pass.
