## When we use it

While the app is open, to show where you are on the map, hatch the local critter and suggest what's nearby. During an active trip, if you turn on travel mode, to notice when you arrive somewhere new and to keep leave-by alarms right.

## What we keep

A visit: the place, the day and roughly how long you stayed. Precise positions used to work that out are deleted within days. We never keep a history of your movements.

## Sharing with your crew

You can share your live location with a crew for a meet-up or a day. It is off by default, shows only to the people you chose, and stops at the time you set.

## Your controls

Change location access in your phone's settings or in the app. With it off, critters wait until you check in by hand and the rest of the app keeps working.
