## What we collect

Your name, email or phone number and profile photo when you sign up. The trips, votes, plans, messages and receipts you add to a crew. Your location while the app is in use or while a trip is active, if you allow it.

We also collect basic device and usage information so we can fix bugs and see which features people use. On this website we count page visits without cookies and without storing anything on your device.

## Location and the pass

Critters hatch when you are actually somewhere, so the pass needs your location to work. We check it when the app is open, and during an active trip if you turn on travel mode.

We store the places you have been as pass entries, not a trail of coordinates. You can turn location off in settings; the rest of the app keeps working. Before you share a location, the app suggests nearby airports from the approximate city of your network address; we don't keep that guess.

## Talking to your guide

When you ask a guide something, by text or voice, we process it to answer you and to update the plan. Voice clips are turned into text and then deleted. The guide is an AI; the [AI guides](/legal/ai) page explains what that means.

Guide conversations are part of your trip. Your crew can see what the guide changed in the plan, not your private questions.

## Who sees what

Everyone in a crew sees that trip: the plan, the votes, shared expenses and the guide's suggestions. People outside the crew see nothing unless you send them an invite or share a link. An invite link shows its crew's name, the first name of whoever sent it and how many people are in, never photos or other members' details.

Your pass is private by default. You can make it visible to friends.

## Partners and services

When you book a stay, activity or transfer through CritterPass, we pass the booking partner the details needed to complete it. Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your card.

We run CritterPass on service providers who process data for us under contract: hosting and databases in Singapore, content delivery, error reporting, cookieless analytics in the EU, email and text-message delivery, and the AI models behind the guides. They may only use your data to provide their service to us.

## Keeping and deleting

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account we remove your profile, pass and private messages within 30 days. Shared trip items you added stay with the crew with your name removed. Records we must keep by law, such as purchase receipts, are kept without your name.

You can delete your account in the app (You → Delete account), or write to [hello@critterpass.app](mailto:hello@critterpass.app) and a person will help.

## Your rights

You can download a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it from Settings → Privacy. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights, and we honour them wherever you are. CritterPass is operated from Singapore and your data is stored there.

## The waitlist

If you joined the waitlist before launch, we hold your email, the destination you picked, your referral handle and when you joined, plus a short-lived hashed record of the network address you signed up from to stop abuse. We use it to tell you when CritterPass opens and to credit referrals, and delete it after launch or when you ask.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. Every version stays readable below.
