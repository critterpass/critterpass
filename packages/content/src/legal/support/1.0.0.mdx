## Getting help

In the app, open You → Help to search answers or message the team. A person reads every message. You can also write to [hello@critterpass.app](mailto:hello@critterpass.app).

## Bookings

For a problem with a stay, ticket or transfer, the partner you booked with can change or cancel it. Open the booking in the app to find their contact details, or ask us and we'll help you reach them.

## Payments and subscriptions

Subscriptions and purchases are managed by the App Store or Google Play; see the [subscription terms](/legal/subscription-terms) for cancelling and refunds.

## Your data and account

Download your data from Settings → Privacy. Delete your account from You → Delete account; your data goes within 30 days, as the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy) explains.

## Reporting something

Report a message, photo or plan by pressing and holding it. For anything urgent about someone's safety, contact local emergency services first.
