## How we're paid

When you book a stay or activity through a link in CritterPass, the partner may pay us a commission. It comes out of their side; the price you pay is the price the partner offers.

## How it shows

Offers from partners appear on partner cards with the partner's name. A sponsored pick is marked "Sponsored", appears at most once in a list, and is never chosen from your personal data. Everywhere else, the order of results doesn't depend on commission.

## Who you book with

We are never the seller. Stays, tickets and transfers are booked with the partner shown, and their terms, cancellation rules and support apply. If something goes wrong with a booking, the partner is the one who can fix it; we will help you reach them.
